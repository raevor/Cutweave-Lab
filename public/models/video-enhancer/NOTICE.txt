Author/weights: **Philip Hofmann**, `2xNomosUni_span_multijpg`, CC BY 4.0. [Author card](https://huggingface.co/Phips/2xNomosUni_span_multijpg), [weights license](NOMOSUNI-SPAN-LICENSE.txt). SPAN architecture: Hongyuan Yu et al., Apache-2.0, [architecture license](SPAN-LICENSE.txt).

## CPU: Qualcomm QuickSRNet Medium 2x

Author: **Qualcomm Innovation Center / Qualcomm Technologies and contributors**. Architecture and official checkpoints: https://github.com/quic/aimet-model-zoo/tree/develop/aimet_zoo_torch/quicksrnet ; https://github.com/qualcomm/ai-hub-models/tree/main/src/qai_hub_models/models/quicksrnetmedium . Paper: https://arxiv.org/abs/2303.04336 . BSD-3-Clause; include [upstream license](QUICKSRNET-LICENSE.txt) and [upstream notice](QUICKSRNET-NOTICE.txt). No endorsement is implied.

Official checkpoint: https://github.com/quic/aimet-model-zoo/releases/download/phase_2_january_artifacts/quicksrnet_medium_2x_checkpoint_float32.pth.tar . SHA256 `a0d176b40a649e45a176c3b53f45e0237015f4f2c17b157ef5c81e38c4442a0d`.

The application packages the official weights as seven Conv/Clip layers followed by CRD pixel shuffle. **Our CPU quantized derivatives** replace those layers with per-channel-weight uint8 QLinearConv using known [0,1] clipped activation ranges and int32 biases. These exact ONNX files are not author-provided exports. Native variants average the clipped predicted subpixels without forming an enlarged image. No training or private calibration media is used. Quantization changes numerical output; visual equivalence or improvement on every source is not established. FP32 QuickSRNet compatibility assets are used if the selected CPU backend cannot run quantized operations.
